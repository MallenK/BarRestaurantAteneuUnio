# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary and confirmed growth priority: tourists (international and domestic) visiting the Cripta Güell / Colònia Güell (UNESCO World Heritage site since 2005, ~5 min walk from the terrace), looking for food or drink before or after the visit. Secondary, still actively served: regular neighborhood clientele from Colònia Güell / Santa Coloma de Cervelló (families, locals having vermut or the menú del día). The redesign should prioritize converting tourist foot traffic without displacing the existing local base.

## Product Purpose

Bar-restaurant serving Mediterranean home cooking — a daily-changing menú del día, classic tapas, hot/cold bocatas, and vermut — on a terrace at Plaça Joan Güell. Success means converting Cripta Güell foot traffic into diners and drinkers, and remaining the trusted local spot for regulars.

## Positioning

The only bar-restaurant with a terrace directly on Plaça Joan Güell, at the physical doorstep of Gaudí's Cripta Güell. Already recommended informally in travel guides as "the terrace for vermut" near the crypt — a location and reputation no competitor can physically replicate. Confirmed competitive gap: Cal Pupinet, a nearby restaurant, currently outranks Ateneu Unió in search for "Cripta Gaudí + Colònia Güell" despite not having this location advantage.

## Operating Context

- Daily-changing menú del día, disclosed in person, not published as a fixed list.
- Fixed-price tapas/bocatas menu across three categories: Tapes, Freds, Calents.
- Vermut terrace culture, especially weekends.
- Reservations handled manually — mostly by phone and email. No automatic confirmation system today.
- Live football screening on the terrace.
- Trilingual clientele in practice: Catalan/Spanish locals, English-speaking tourists.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Reservations: phone and email are the current, working process. Whether to automate this in the redesign is an open decision — not confirmed either way, do not assume a booking engine is wanted.
- The menú del día changes daily and cannot be hardcoded as a static list; only the tapas/bocatas categories have stable, listable pricing.
- Cancellation policy: the business reserves the right to cancel a booking.
- No in-house design/dev team; the current site is maintained by the owner and deployed via GitHub Pages.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ateneu Unió (Bar Restaurant Ateneu Unió).
- Existing simple wordmark/monogram: "AU".
- Trilingual presence: Catalan (default), Spanish, English.
- Address: Plaça Joan Güell, 5, Colònia Güell, 08690.

## Evidence on Hand

- Informal phone/social photos of the bar, dishes, and team exist (not professional quality) — usable as interim reference while a professional shoot is arranged. No professional photography exists yet; all imagery on the current site is third-party stock (Wikimedia Commons) of the building and crypt, none of the actual bar or food.
- Three existing customer reviews (currently embedded as JSON-LD) praising the home cooking, "braves," croquettes, and terrace ambience.
- Competitor evidence: Cal Pupinet publishes content targeting "Cripta Gaudí + Colònia Güell" search intent that Ateneu Unió does not yet contest.
- No case studies, press mentions, or third-party awards on hand — must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Physical proximity to the Cripta Güell is the strategic asset — the site's job is to convert that foot traffic into visits, not just describe the menu.
2. Growth comes from tourism layered on top of the existing local base, not by replacing the neighborhood identity.
3. Reservations stay a manual, personal process unless the owner decides otherwise — design must support phone/email as the primary path, not assume automation.
4. Once real material exists, content and photography must represent the actual place — no stock imagery standing in for the real bar, food, or terrace.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific standard mandated by the user. The current implementation already applies ARIA labels, keyboard-operable tabs, and visible focus states — preserve this baseline going forward.
